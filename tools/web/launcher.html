Bellows

Your line snaps at the bottom of a shaft and you land in Wick, a lamp-lit town built on a dead pumping station. Grow crops in the dark, look under the streets through the surveyor's plumb-glass at the pipes, wires, water and air that keep it breathing, and get to know the people who remember what you do. Then open the old valve, and find out who has been knocking back from below all these years.
